Chip refinement directions

Same mechanics, three visual theses. Each shows the live league chip, the in-play moment chip, and a final chip — on comfort (56px) and compact (36px) rails. 1b is my recommendation.

1a

The chip is a tiny TV scorebug: two team rows, leader bold, win% as its own zone. Structure over text soup.

1b
Spine — score is the hero, win odds are ambient Recommended
One big number per chip, one quiet line of context, and a 2px win-probability spine along the bottom edge. Every state is the same shape.

  • Score at 15px is the biggest number on the rail — glanceable from across the screen
  • The spine makes win odds ambient: fill = your chances, full bar = final, empty = pre. Player chips reuse it for progress vs projection
  • Quietest ink; most Scrollr-like; narrowest live chip of the three
